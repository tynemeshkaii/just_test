Real stories from real people — and real cars.

Porsche 911 Carrera 4S
★★★★★
Heritage / OEM

It doesn't shout "wrap." It looks like a heritage edition Porsche could have built at the factory — subtle until the light catches the lines.

D
Daniel W.
Dubai

I've owned a few 911s, and the last thing I wanted was anything that screamed aftermarket. I asked for something restrained — heritage stripes that respect the car's lines instead of fighting them.

The team studied the 992 body and proposed a layout that follows the shoulder line and sits exactly where a factory designer would put it: crisp edges, perfect symmetry, colours chosen to work with the paint.

The result looks like a limited edition that left Stuttgart that way. People assume it's a factory option — and that's the highest compliment I could ask for.

Ferrari Roma
★★★★★
Tailor Made / Elegant

One understated accent line — and the Roma went from beautiful to unmistakable. It reads as a Tailor Made finish, not a sticker.

M
Marco V.
Dubai

The Roma is an elegant car, and I was nervous about adding anything to it. I didn't want graphics — I wanted a single detail done to Ferrari's own standard.

The team suggested one fine accent line running through the profile, in a tone picked to match the interior stitching. Minimal, precise, almost invisible until you notice it.

That one detail changed the whole car. It now has a quiet signature that looks like it came from the Tailor Made department. Friends with Ferraris keep asking which option it is.

Lamborghini Urus
★★★★★
Statement / Premium

Bold enough to suit a Lamborghini, refined enough to never look cheap. The Urus finally looks as dramatic as it drives.

R
Rashid A.
Dubai

The Urus already turns heads, but I wanted it to feel like a special edition rather than a standard configurator build. The challenge was going bold without crossing into tasteless.

We developed a graphic package that wraps the lower body and follows the aggressive lines Lamborghini built into the car — strong, but balanced and perfectly aligned.

The finish is premium in person; the edges and fitment are flawless. It looks dramatic, expensive, and intentional — exactly the statement I wanted.

Mercedes-AMG G 63
★★★★★
Desert / Luxury

Desert character with a luxury finish. The G 63 looks like a limited Dubai edition — tough, but unmistakably high-end.

K
Khalid M.
Abu Dhabi

The G 63 is the perfect canvas, but most builds I see are either too plain or far too loud. I wanted something with desert character that still felt luxury, not military cosplay.

The team designed side graphics in a sand-toned palette that suits the region, placed to emphasise the boxy lines rather than hide them — subtle texture, premium materials.

It now looks like a limited Dubai edition. Rugged enough to mean it, refined enough that it belongs outside any five-star hotel.

BMW M4 Coupe
★★★★★
M Motorsport / OEM

Factory M Motorsport spirit, done properly. The M4 looks like something BMW's own M division would have signed off on.

M
Mikhail R.
Dubai

I wanted the M4 to wear the M Motorsport colours, but I've seen too many that look like a cheap decal kit from the internet — the placement is always slightly off and it ruins the whole thing.

These guys treated it like an OEM job: correct tricolour tones, exact placement along the body lines, perfect symmetry left to right. It extends the car's design instead of sitting on top of it.

The M4 now looks like a genuine M Motorsport edition. Even fellow BMW owners assume it's a factory package — exactly the reaction I was after.

Porsche 718 Cayman
★★★★★
GT / Track-look

Track-day attitude without the boy-racer look. The graphics give the Cayman a GT presence that holds up next to far more expensive cars.

S
Sebastian H.
Dubai

The Cayman is a proper driver's car, and I wanted it to look the part on track days without turning into a rolling sticker bomb — tasteful, motorsport-inspired, but clean enough for the school run.

We went with subtle GT-style accents along the sides and front, sized and placed to match the 982 proportions. Nothing oversized, nothing random.

What impressed me was the restraint. Up close it reads as a serious track car, but from across the lot it just looks like a sharper, more purposeful Cayman.

Design philosophy

It’s about belonging, not just decoration

01

Material

High-grade laminated vinyl engineered for Dubai heat and sun. Holds colour and edges for years — no fading, no lifting, no "aftermarket" look.

02

Color

Every shade is matched to your exact paint tone. The right combination reads factory; the wrong one reads cheap from 10 metres.

03

Placement

Each curve, recess and panel wrapped by hand. The line between OEM-level and "sticker on a car" lives in these edges.

04

Proportion

Size, angle and position chosen for your specific model. A few centimeters decide whether it looks deliberate or added-on.
