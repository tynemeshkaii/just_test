# Just Graphics — Landing Reimagine (Premium-Qualified Funnel)

**Date:** 2026-06-23
**Goal:** Stop attracting cheap, low-intent leads from Instagram/Facebook ads. Reposition the landing(s) to attract fewer but higher-quality, solvent leads who genuinely want our work.
**Direction chosen:** A+C hybrid — *Premium Craft Reposition* (evolution of existing structure) + *Qualified Funnel* (price transparency + multi-step qualifying brief).
**Scope:** Both pages (`index.html`, `dealers/index.html`) via shared assets. Reskin + remessage + new form. **Not** a ground-up rebuild.

---

## 1. Strategic context

- **Problem:** Current page is built to *maximize lead volume* — "free quote", one-tap WhatsApp, no price signal, urgency copy, gold/badge/counter spam, ricer-tuner builds on display. Result: max clicks, min intent, leads with no budget.
- **Core principle:** To raise lead quality, **filter, not maximize.** Add qualifying friction + signal price. Fewer leads, far higher intent + budget. **CPL will rise — that is correct.** Measure cost per *qualified* lead, not raw CPL.
- **Reference (client-supplied):** thetopazshop.com — approachable-premium: restraint, whitespace, no begging, trust via guarantee, zero urgency. Steal its *poise*, not its e-commerce model (we sell a bespoke service, not £9 products).
- **Real economics:** typical project **AED 1,500–5,000**. Target is enthusiasts + nicer-car owners who pay for quality and won't haggle — **not** ultra-HNW atelier, **not** AED-200 sticker shoppers. Filter goal = screen out bargain-hunters/hagglers.
- **Constraint:** existing photography only. Premium signal must come from layout, type, whitespace, restraint — not new shots.

## 2. Positioning & messaging spine

- **Brand idea:** Bespoke automotive graphics — designed, printed, fitted to your exact car, under one roof in Dubai. Craft + precision. Approachable-premium.
- **Core promise:** A design made for *your* car, approved by you before anything is printed, in premium removable material that leaves the paint untouched.
- **4 value pillars (each also a quality filter):**
  1. **Fitted, not generic** — adapted to your model's panels/curves, mm precision (vs peel-and-stick).
  2. **Premium material, clean removal** — no residue, no paint damage, inspected first.
  3. **You approve the concept before we print** — de-risks the spend.
  4. **One roof, full service** — design → print → install, RTA handled.
- **Tone:** confident, calm, specific. Kill urgency + "free" + "today/same-day."

**Vocabulary swaps:**

| Out (cheap signal) | In (premium signal) |
|---|---|
| decal / sticker | graphics / livery / design |
| Get a Free Quote | Start your project / Request a consultation |
| today, same-day, hurry | considered, precise, by appointment |
| 1,500+ cars done (loud, volume-only) | 17 years · 1,500+ cars · Ferrari to Land Cruiser (static, mastery-framed) |

**Price framing (the filter):** "Projects from AED 1,500. Most clients invest AED 1,500–5,000, depending on coverage and complexity."

**Audience-filter line:** "For owners who want it done properly — once."

## 3. Social proof — keep 1,500+, reframe as mastery

Volume stays; change its *costume* from carnival counter to engraved credential.
1. **Always pair volume with the time denominator:** "17 years · 1,500+ cars" (~88/yr = steady craft, not churn).
2. **Anchor upmarket with marque range:** "from daily drivers to Ferrari, Lamborghini and Porsche."
3. **Kill the animated count-up.** Present static, quiet.

**Placement:**
- Hero: one quiet inline line — "17 years. 1,500+ cars. 5.0 on Google."
- Proof strip (new) after hero: "17 years · 1,500+ cars · 5.0 Google · Ferrari to Land Cruiser."
- Why-us: expanded — "Over 17 years we've transformed 1,500+ cars across Dubai — from everyday builds to exotics."
- NOT a giant bouncing number competing with the CTA.

## 4. Page structure (both pages)

Proposed order (current → proposed):

1. **Nav** — CTA "Start your project" (reframe from "Get a Free Quote")
2. **Hero** — repositioned, Direction A (editorial calm), qualifying CTA
3. **Proof strip** — *new*, static credential
4. **Work / Cases** — before/after, curated best, premium captions
5. **Why bespoke** — 4 value pillars (reframe)
6. **Process** — *moved up*; "you approve before we print" must land before the ask
7. **Gallery** — reframed "starting points we tailor" (not "39 ready designs")
8. **Reviews** — *recurated*, premium marques first
9. **Price + qualifying brief** — the C filter
10. **FAQ** — premium tone (price/material/removal)
11. **Footer** — studio, by appointment, map

**4 structural moves that matter:** ① quiet proof strip after hero; ② Process moved up (trust before ask); ③ Gallery reframed off the "sticker menu"; ④ dedicated Price + Brief section.

## 5. Hero — Direction A (Editorial calm)

- Existing Ferrari shot, darkened gradient, content bottom-left.
- Eyebrow: "Bespoke Automotive Graphics · Dubai"
- Headline (serif): **"Make your car unmistakably yours."** (alternatives noted: "Built for you, fitted to your car" / "Graphics worthy of the car you drive")
- Sub: "Designed, printed and fitted to your exact car — under one roof. You approve the concept before we print."
- Quiet credential line: "17 years · 1,500+ cars · ★ 5.0 Google"
- CTAs: primary "Start your project" (→ Section 09 brief); WhatsApp as quiet secondary.
- No urgency, no badge spam, gold as thin accent only.

## 6. Price tiers + qualifying brief (Section 09 — the filter)

**Transparent price tiers** (shown above the form):
- Accent & partial — *from AED 1,500*
- Multi-panel / livery — *AED 3,000–5,000*
- Full custom build — *AED 5,000+*

**Multi-step brief (4 steps, chosen over single form):**
1. **Your car** — make / model / year
2. **Vision** — ready style / custom design / not sure
3. **Budget** — AED 1,500–3,000 · 3,000–5,000 · 5,000+ · "Not sure — advise me" *(the qualifier)*
4. **Contact** — name + phone/WhatsApp

- Budget step is the hard qualifier. Captured to Bitrix + passed to Meta CAPI as event value for value-based optimization.
- WhatsApp remains a quiet secondary path for form-refusers.

## 7. Proof & curation

- **Reviews:** lead with Porsche 911 GT3; demote/cut budget-tuner builds (Supra JDM, 370Z drift, Patrol) — they signal "ricer shop." Tasteful builds only.
- **Cases:** keep Ferrari / Bronco / Lambo. Premium captions — e.g. "Factory-grade Bronco graphics, fitted in-house" (not "without the dealer wait").
- **Gallery:** "39 ready designs" → "Starting points — each tailored to your car." Same images, reframed.

## 8. Aesthetic system (detune)

**Remove (cheap tells):** gold-on-everything; animated count-ups; urgency copy; badge-spam pills; shouting floating WA + sticky bar; "Free" everywhere; dense layout.

**Replace (studio restraint):** gold as thin accent only (rules/marks); static engraved credentials; calm specific copy; one quiet credential line; soft single CTA + WhatsApp as quiet link; "Start your project"/"Request a consultation"; generous whitespace, fewer elements per view.

**Tokens:** keep dark base (premium); shift gold from *fill* → *accent*; add warm off-white (`#F4EFE6`) for editorial space; more line-height + margins; serif reserved for headlines. Base ink `#0A0A09`, panel `#15140f`, gold accent `#C9A84C`.

## 9. Ad-funnel alignment (media side — must accompany)

Landing alone can't fix a cheap funnel. Ad creative + targeting must match:
- **Creative:** finished premium builds + craft/process, not "FREE QUOTE" overlays. Outcome/identity, not discount.
- **Copy:** drop "free/cheap/today"; mirror "bespoke, fitted, from AED 1,500."
- **Price in creative** (recommended): "from AED 1,500" pre-filters before the click — biggest CPL-quality lever.
- **Targeting:** value-based / lookalikes seeded from high-budget leads once the budget field feeds Meta.

## 10. Measurement

- Stop optimizing raw leads/CPL. Expect CPL to rise (correct).
- **North-star:** cost per *qualified* lead (budget ≥ AED 3k) + lead→paid conversion rate.
- Feed Meta the budget tier as event value → optimize toward payers.
- Tag lead quality in Bitrix (budget field); weekly loop — which ad/creative brought qualified leads.

## 11. Technical implementation

- **Both pages via shared assets.** Most work in `assets/site.css` + `assets/site.js` (applies to both). Per-page: head meta + inline `window.JG_pageInit` blocks (catalog/forms differ).
- **Multi-step brief:** new shared module; replaces current single lead form (`#page-lead-form` / `#lead-form-sa`). Posts to existing Worker.
- **Worker change** (`worker/src/index.js`, deployed `just-graphics-leads`): accept + forward `budget` + `coverage` → Bitrix lead field (`crm.lead.add` mapping) + Meta CAPI value param. Additive, small.
- **Keep** clean architecture (tokens, scroll-reveal, sliders, IntersectionObserver). Reskin + remessage + new form, not a rebuild.
- Update `AGENTS.md`/`CLAUDE.md` if structure changes.

## 12. Phasing

- **P1 (fast, high impact):** copy rewrite + price anchor + CTA swap (form-primary/WA-secondary) + recurate reviews/cases/gallery captions.
- **P2:** aesthetic detune (gold→accent, remove urgency/counters/badge-spam, whitespace).
- **P3:** multi-step qualifying brief + Worker `budget`/`coverage` field + Meta value optimization.
- **P4 (media side):** ad-creative alignment + value-based targeting.

## 13. Success criteria

- Lower share of "no-budget"/haggler leads (qualitative from sales).
- Higher cost per *qualified* lead is acceptable; lead→paid conversion rate up.
- Meta optimizing on budget-tier value; Bitrix leads carry budget field.
- Page reads as an established studio, not an ad funnel (subjective + bounce/scroll-depth on premium traffic).

## 14. Out of scope / YAGNI

- No new photography (existing only).
- No ground-up rebuild; no framework/build tooling.
- No e-commerce/checkout (Topaz model not adopted).
- Pure editorial showcase (Direction B) rejected — photography constraint + both-page scope.
